Role of urban local bodies

After the 74th Constitutional Amendment (1992), municipalities are responsible for local services and urban poverty alleviation.

Identify & register

Ward offices and municipal corporations survey vendors and workers, issue vending certificates and help with registration camps.

Facilitate schemes

Urban local bodies (ULBs), Common Service Centres and ward committees help workers enrol, collect documents and resolve grievances.

Provide spaces & services

Vending zones, shelters, sanitation, crèches and skill centres improve working conditions for informal workers.

Participation

Ward committees, town vending committees and worker unions give informal workers a voice in city planning.

Frequently asked questions

Is registration free?

Yes. e-Shram registration is free, and no one should charge you for it. Check with your CSC or ward office for any official fees on other services.

Can I join PMSYM and e-Shram both?

Yes. e-Shram is a registration database and a gateway; PMSYM is a pension scheme you can enrol in once you meet its criteria.

What if I stop paying PMSYM contributions?

If you stop before 10 years, you generally get back your contribution with savings-bank interest. After 10 years, you can continue paying until age 60 or exit as per scheme rules.

Do I need an Aadhaar card?

Aadhaar is the main ID for e-Shram and PMSYM. For Jan Dhan accounts, other officially valid documents are also accepted.

I migrated to a new city. Can I still get benefits?

e-Shram is designed to be portable across states. Contact your new ward office or CSC to update your details.
